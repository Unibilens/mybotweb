# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Discord server owners, community admins, and moderators who want an active, thriving server without manual role management.
- Discord community members who want visual recognition, level progression, and friendly competition.

## Product Purpose

Husky is a Discord activity and leveling bot that tracks member text and voice activity, calculates XP, automatically awards Discord role milestones, and displays leaderboards.

## Positioning

A lightweight, reliable, zero-bloat leveling bot with seamless dual text + voice tracking and automatic role rewards, without hidden paywalls on core leveling features.

## Operating Context

- Web presence: Marketing & invite landing page hosted at `bilendev.net` (GitHub Pages).
- Bot operation: Runs in Discord servers through Discord API and official slash commands (`/rank`, `/leaderboard`, `/rewards`, `/help`).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Capabilities:
  - Text chat XP tracking with anti-spam cooldowns.
  - Voice channel time XP calculation.
  - Automated role assignment on level-up.
  - Interactive `/rank` and `/leaderboard` commands.
- Constraints:
  - Static frontend (HTML5, modern CSS, vanilla JS).
  - Dynamic invite and support links managed via `config.js`.
  - Responsive across all viewports (mobile, tablet, desktop).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Husky
- Avatar: Husky dog mascot (`husky.png` / circular `favicon.png`).
- Core links: Driven by `config.js`.
- Legal pages: `/terms` and `/privacy`.

## Product Principles

1. Zero friction: Visitors should understand what Husky does and invite it in under 30 seconds.
2. Honest utility: Focus on real bot functionality (XP, roles, commands) without fake statistics or fabricated reviews.
3. Accessible & performant: Fast loading, responsive, accessible contrast, smooth non-janky interactions.
