Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective Date: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how Husky ("Bot", "we", "us", "our") collects, processes, stores, and safeguards information obtained from users and Discord servers ("guilds") in full compliance with the Discord Developer Policy, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and applicable international privacy regulations.

🛡️ CRITICAL PRIVACY GUARANTEE:
Husky strictly respects your privacy. We NEVER read, collect, inspect, or store the actual text content of your messages, uploaded attachments, images, or media. We NEVER record, listen to, or process audio communications in Discord voice channels. All activity calculations are performed strictly on numerical timestamps and event counts.

1. Information We Collect

To provide activity tracking, level roles, and leaderboard features, Husky processes only the minimum data technically required:

  • Public Discord Identifiers:
    • User ID, Username, and Avatar Hash: Necessary to identify your account, associate experience points (XP), and render rank cards / leaderboards.
    • Guild (Server) ID, Channel IDs, and Role IDs: Necessary to associate configurations, role rewards, and blacklisted channels with specific servers.
  • Activity Metrics (Numerical Only):
    • Message count and timestamps (used solely to calculate message XP).
    • Voice channel connection duration and timestamps (used solely to calculate voice XP).
    • Invite counts and bonus point allocations configured by server admins.
  • Server Configurations: Custom settings chosen by server administrators, including XP multipliers, level-up notification channels, and custom role assignments.

2. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR & International Law)

We process your data under the legal basis of Legitimate Interest and Performance of a Contract to provide the core requested services (tracking server activity, generating leaderboards, and distributing configured level roles) when Husky is added to a server.

3. How We Use the Data

Collected data is used exclusively to:

  • Calculate user levels, experience points (XP), and rankings.
  • Automatically assign or revoke server-configured level roles.
  • Display server leaderboards via /top, /leaderboard, and /rank commands.
  • Enforce cooldowns and protect system stability against abuse.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data with any third party, advertiser, or data broker under any circumstances.

Data is only processed through the official Discord API as necessary for bot operations, governed by Discord's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Storage & Security Measures

All collected data is stored in isolated, access-restricted database systems protected by modern security practices, including network firewalls, encrypted connections, and access control. We take all reasonable and appropriate technical measures to prevent unauthorized access, accidental loss, alteration, or disclosure of data.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Rights (GDPR Article 17 / Right to Erasure)

We retain data only for as long as necessary to maintain active server leaderboards:

  • Server Data Wiping: Server administrators can instantly delete and purge all server-specific activity data, XP logs, and leaderboards using the /reset command.
  • Individual User Deletion: Any individual user or server administrator has the right to request complete deletion of their stored data from our database. To exercise your Right to Erasure, simply join our Support Server and submit a deletion request. Requests are processed within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Husky is not directed toward children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under these age limits. If we discover that a user under the legal age has provided personal data, we will take immediate steps to delete such data from our systems.

8. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." Continued use of Husky after updates are published signifies acceptance of the revised policy.
