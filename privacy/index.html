Privacy Policy

Effective & Last Updated: September 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy outlines how Husky ("Bot", "we", "us", "our") collects, processes, stores, and safeguards information obtained from users and Discord servers ("guilds") in compliance with the Discord Developer Policy, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and applicable international privacy regulations.

🛡️ Core Privacy Guarantee

Husky strictly respects your privacy. We never read, collect, inspect, or store the content of your messages, uploaded attachments, images, or media files. We never record, listen to, or process audio communications in Discord voice channels. All activity calculations are performed strictly on numerical timestamps and event counts.

1. Information We Process

To provide activity tracking, level roles, and leaderboard features, Husky processes only the minimum data required for operation:

  • Public Discord Identifiers:
    • User ID, Username, and Avatar Hash: Necessary to identify accounts, associate experience points (XP), and render rank cards and leaderboards.
    • Guild (Server) ID, Channel IDs, and Role IDs: Necessary to associate configurations, role rewards, and blacklisted channels with specific servers.
  • Activity Metrics (Numerical Only):
    • Message count and timestamps (used solely to calculate message XP).
    • Voice channel connection duration and timestamps (used solely to calculate voice XP).
    • Invite counts and bonus point allocations configured by server admins.
  • Server Configurations: Custom settings chosen by server administrators, including XP multipliers, level-up notification channels, and custom role assignments.

2. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

We process data under the legal basis of Legitimate Interest and Performance of a Contract to provide the core requested services (tracking server activity, generating leaderboards, and distributing configured level roles) when Husky is added to a Discord server.

3. How We Use the Data

Collected data is used exclusively to:

  • Calculate user levels, experience points (XP), and rankings.
  • Automatically assign or revoke server-configured level roles upon milestone completion.
  • Display server leaderboards via /leaderboard, /rank, and related commands.
  • Enforce cooldowns and protect system stability against automated abuse.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade personal data with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers under any circumstances.

Data is processed through the official Discord API as necessary for bot operations, governed by Discord's Privacy Policy.

5. Data Storage & Security Measures

All collected data is stored in isolated, access-restricted database systems protected by industry-standard security practices, including network firewalls, encrypted connections, and access control. We take all reasonable technical measures to prevent unauthorized access, accidental loss, alteration, or disclosure of data.

6. Data Retention & Deletion Rights (GDPR Article 17)

We retain data only for as long as necessary to maintain active server leaderboards:

  • Server Data Reset: Server administrators can purge server-specific activity data, XP logs, and leaderboards using the /reset command.
  • Individual User Deletion: Any user or server administrator has the right to request complete deletion of their stored data. To exercise your Right to Erasure, join our Support Server and submit a request. Requests are processed within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Husky is not directed toward children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under these age limits. If we discover that a user under the legal age has provided personal data, we take immediate steps to delete such data from our systems.

8. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." Continued use of Husky after updates are published signifies acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact & Support

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to submit a data removal request, please contact us via our official Discord Support Server.
